# GRIDSOFT

GRIDSOFT Ltd. is a Belizean technology company. We design, develop and license software, hardware and the technology that connects them, and we run what we build ourselves.

Everything is engineered in-house, from the servers to the screens, so the people who answer for a product are the people who built it.

We build regulated products the regulated way. We work with Belize's financial regulators, and we do not offer any service that needs a licence until we hold it.
